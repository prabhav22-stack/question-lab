## 1. Methodological Notes on Question Classification

1. **Multi-Subtopic Hybrid Questions:**
   Certain historical essay questions intentionally bridge two subtopics (e.g. comparing Maslow and Herzberg in MPA-012 U12, or linking Recruitment and Promotion in MPA-014 U05). In every such instance:
   - Exactly one **Primary Subtopic** was assigned based on the core conceptual proposition.
   - The secondary theme was preserved as `secondary_subtopic_id`.
   - The question is printed under its primary subtopic to prevent double-counting in aggregate historical counts.

2. **Split 10-Mark Notes (Questions 5 & 10):**
   Where Question 5 or Question 10 contains subparts (a) and (b), each part carries 10 marks and was classified as `Split Short Note (10M)` requiring a concise 250-word answer.

3. **Essays Phrased as Notes:**
   Exactly 22 questions carried 20 marks but opened with phrasing such as "Write a note on...". These were explicitly flagged and verified as requiring full 500-word structured essays.
